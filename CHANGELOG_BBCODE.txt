[SIZE="2"][b][COLOR="RoyalBlue"]Memory Cleanup:[/COLOR][/b][/SIZE]
[LIST]
[*] [COLOR="Lime"]Added[/COLOR] a [COLOR="Lime"]low-memory callback[/COLOR] on [COLOR="#FF69B4"]console[/COLOR], fired when the game warns that add-on memory is low, plus a check of how close the add-on memory is to its limit.
[*] [COLOR="Lime"]Added[/COLOR] a [COLOR="Lime"]shared low-memory rescue[/COLOR] on [COLOR="#FF69B4"]console[/COLOR]: it cleans in small steps every frame until add-on memory is back under [COLOR="Orange"]65%[/COLOR] of the limit, adds a [COLOR="Lime"]Deep Clean[/COLOR] above [COLOR="Orange"]80%[/COLOR] when the steps fall behind, and keeps going in combat. Only the add-on that calls it starts it, and a second add-on joins the running one.
[*] [COLOR="#1E90FF"]Updated[/COLOR] cleanup results to say why the cleanup ran [COLOR="Gray"][i](for example "lowmem" for the [COLOR="#FF69B4"]console[/COLOR]'s [COLOR="#1E90FF"]Low Add-On memory warning[/COLOR])[/i][/COLOR].
[/LIST]
